## OUR STORY

It’s been said that meetings and events have literally made the world go around. From the last supper to the very first US constitutional convention to the investigator meetings that resulted in the groundbreaking cancer treatments of today, meetings and events have been brining people together to effect monumental, positive change. Limestone meetings and events prescribes to the notion that, when you bring people together you can change the world. Whether your objectives are centered around training, strategic goal setting, long-term planning or any number of other initiatives that allow your business to run more effectively, our team is dedicated to providing event support that allows you to focus to rest upon harnessing the richness of the outcome.

​

Limestone meetings and events is a nexus of decades of specialized events experience, strategic leadership consulting and a vision for agile event solutions that truly can be a catalyst.
