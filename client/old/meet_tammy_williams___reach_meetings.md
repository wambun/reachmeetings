## Tammy Williams   Vice President of Strategic Accounts

![tammy.heic](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/28cd78_e43a3acc9022444997774babb647bfab~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_220,h_321,al_c,q_85,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/tammy_heic.png)

Tammy has worked in sales, marketing and customer service her entire career.  She transitioned from the corporate sector to hospitality in 2005 where her skills were honed in group sales while fostering her passion for all things travel.

In 2016, Tammy began her work on the meeting and event planning side of the industry, specializing in medical meeting sales. Her approach has always been simple, to treat others the way she wishes to be treated.  She is driven to help others and is always seeking opportunities to be a conduit in meeting needs and adding value to her clients and coworkers alike.

​

Tammy and her husband Rickey have three sons, two grandbabies and the meanest, most loved Shih Tzu in Texas named Coco. They spend their free time traveling, often RV'ing across the country, at the pickleball courts or just playing board games at home in north Texas. Their family motto is, "Life is short. Fill it up," and they certainly do.
