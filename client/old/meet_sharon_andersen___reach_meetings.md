## Sharon Andersen, CMM   Senior Planner

![selfiev5.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/28cd78_bf01c30e602f43e29c9a6b251e6e952c~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_205,h_301,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/selfiev5.jpg)

Sharon Andersen brings over two decades of global leadership experience.  She possesses deep expertise in event marketing strategy, design, planning, and management.  Sharon has a wealth of industry knowledge as well as strong program and people management skills supporting a variety of industries including high-tech, banking, and non-profit organizations. She has built her success by focusing on the customer experience with a highly effective collaborative and influential relationship-building approach.

Sharon's global expertise is with large and small-scale conferences, executive (high-touch) events, sales incentive programs, trade shows, award ceremonies, meetings, and events. From a 9,500-person customer conference to an executive dinner in the Tower of London to a 5-day custom wine country retreat, to a  2,000-person private concert - she has had the pleasure of designing and creating many memorable events!

Sharon holds a B.S. in Psychology as well as her Certificate of Meeting Manager Designation.  She currently serves on the board of advisors for Market Ready Index, a one-of-a-kind marketing assessment tool dedicated to identifying marketing program effectiveness.

In 2022, Sharon received her Level One Certification from the Court of Master Sommeliers Americas.  Additionally, she holds an IATA Travel designation as well as the COVID Compliance Officer designation.
