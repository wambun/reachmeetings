## Nora Bakke   Senior Planner

![Nora Bakke Headshot.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/28cd78_550014d057bb46ea899c585a400f3d49~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_160,h_301,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/Nora%20Bakke%20Headshot.jpg)

I have worked over 15 years in meeting & event planning. Having a successful meeting involves a strong collaboration with clients, peers and vendors. I have managed Pharma Investigator Meetings and Advisory Board Meetings in the USA, Latin America, Asia, Australia and Europe. I am happy to add another continent. Let’s go!

​

My work history starts in hotel hospitality. In my mind, the best training to prepare a person in this industry is to work at the front desk at a busy hotel. Throw in the graveyard shift! For over a decade, I have worked in the entertainment business. I consider myself fortunate to have developed strong relationships throughout my professional journey.

Outside of work, I enjoy time with friends and family which includes several fur babies. I am known to pull over when I see a stray dog. I also help rescue and TNR cats. The neighborhood kids are now calling me the Cat Lady.
