## DeeDee Bailey   Senior Planner

![DeeDee Bailey.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/28cd78_e744fa2fd409455e9985f714228ddace~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_160,h_301,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/DeeDee%20Bailey.jpg)

I have a passion for planning that has led me on a dynamic career of managing and implementing a wide array of events both nationally and internationally.  My attention to detail and exceptional customer service has helped me create memorable experiences for my partners.  Throughout my career I have developed an in-depth skill set that allows for me to smoothly execute all aspects of an event. There is no better feeling than to see my events meet and exceed client expectations.

​

Most of my career has been managing large technical conferences and exhibitions for up to 100,000 people.   During this time, I spear-headed an organization’s first international convention in Florence, Italy.   I have also handled a variety of private events from client appreciation receptions to fundraising galas.

​

My current and past volunteer experience includes Junior League of Dallas, The American Red Cross of Dallas, Elan Circle of the Dallas Symphony Orchestra League, Ability Connection, Richardson ISD and St. Mark’s School of Texas.

​

I live in Dallas with my husband and two children.  In my spare time I enjoy working out, reading, travelling, and spending time with family and friends.
