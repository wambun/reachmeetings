## Kate Miller, CMP   Senior Planner

![DSC00266-4-16 (192x240).jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/28cd78_f76416bdb4ac4edc9ef97034c8ec3a50~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_219,h_274,al_c,lg_1,q_80,enc_avif,quality_auto/DSC00266-4-16%20(192x240).jpg)

Kate has 17 years of experience in strategic planning and production of business events, educational conferences, experiential environments, marketing activations and program management.

She has extensive creative agency experience partnering with leading brands and organizations to produce conferences, tradeshows, marketing activations and client appreciation events.

Prior to her work as an event professional, Kate held a wide range of management roles in the hospitality industry including front office, food & beverage, sales and catering.

Kate is motivated by every aspect of the event spectrum from developing the fundamental strategy that drives the best results, to planning critical details, to event execution and realizing meaningful outcomes.

​

Kate and her husband live in Austin, Texas.  She has two grown stepchildren.  In her free time Kate appreciates an authentic travel experience, a good book, cooking for her family and friends and spending quality time in the great outdoors.
