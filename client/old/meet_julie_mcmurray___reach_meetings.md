## Julie McMurray   Senior Planner

![IMG_2963_edited.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/28cd78_11cb330720db486a96c00446cf2d76c0~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_213,h_313,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/IMG_2963_edited.jpg)

Julie McMurray brings 39 years of professional experience in the meeting and incentive travel industry to the REACH Team.

Her roles and responsibilities have included a client-focused approach in the management of a wide-range of full-service meeting and incentives globally to include sourcing, team/meeting management, on-site management, meeting planning, site selection, group air, private charters, speakers, entertainment, production, ground transportation, reconciliation and online registration-CVENT.

Julie's wealth of successful experience in working with incentives, executive and special event groups include a variety of clients in the financial, insurance, medical, pharmaceutical, network services, sports, technology, social media, distributions, marketing, retail, manufacturing, and agriculture divisions.

Julie has served on five Meeting Industry Advisory Boards and Board Chair for the Midwest FICP Organization. She has a heart for community, volunteering for United Way, Shoes that Fit, St. Jude’s and Red Cross.

She lives in Ankeny, Iowa with her husband. Together, they have six children and six grandchildren and enjoy family, boating, snowmobiling and traveling!
