## Ginger Murchison   Senior Planner

![Ginger_edited.png](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/28cd78_1ffa9c691d3a42e995fa0270c477c1f4~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_200,h_200,al_c,q_85,enc_avif,quality_auto/Ginger_edited.png)

Ginger has 20 years of experience in global meeting management, incentive event experiences, sports hospitality & destination management.  As a Senior Program Manager at Fan Fares, Ginger planned dozens of Sports Hospitality programs for corporate clients at the SuperBowl, Masters, and Final Four.

She has extensive planning and production agency experience partnering with organizations to produce annual meetings, conferences, tradeshows, marketing activations and client appreciation events.

Select clients include Wells Fargo (annual incentive meetings, 5-star properties), Bank of America (Masters programs), FritoLay, Philip Morris, and Global Pharmaceutical Companies.
