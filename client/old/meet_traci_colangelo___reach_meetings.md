## Traci Colangelo   Senior Sourcing Specialist & Travel Manager

![Traci Colangelo.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/28cd78_888c30f569a84779a318e562dd11d9ba~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_204,h_301,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/Traci%20Colangelo.jpg)

I have 15 plus years in the hospitality industry with experience in operations, reservations, revenue management, and sales.  Over the years in the industry, I have worked for hotels in Southern California, Arizona and Southeast Texas. I am proud to have been recognized as Manager of the Year.

My most recent experience has been sourcing and meeting management with a private meeting management company.  In this position, I was able to negotiate monetary savings for my clients during contract negotiations with hotels, and my prior experience in hotels greatly assisted me in this ability.  I am proud to have received several accolades in this position as well.

​

While my husband was in the Army, I volunteered as the company Family Readiness Group leader and greatly enjoyed assisting families during times of deployment.

I have grown up and lived all over the southern United States from east to west coast. I currently live in Montgomery, Texas just north of Houston, Texas with my husband, two sons, and our dog. I enjoy working on home renovation projects, gardening, traveling and spending time with my family and friends.
