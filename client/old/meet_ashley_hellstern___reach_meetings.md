Exceptional customer service is my ultimate goal. There is nothing better professionally than being on the receiving end of a huge smile and true appreciation. I have a vast knowledge of the hospitality industry from my 15+ years of hotel and sourcing experience. Protecting my clients is top priority so negotiating the best terms and conditions is second nature to me. I truly love when I can get the best deal for my customer and exceed their expectations.

​

When I’m not exceeding expectations at work, I’m probably running after my two boys Finn and Ryan or outside enjoying nature with my amazing husband, Russ, and our ½ Schnauzer - ½ crazy dog, Annie. Some of my favorite things are gardening, listening to 50’s music, especially blues and jazz and anything related to WWII history. I’m a true New Orleanian, so my philosophy is, “Let the good times roll!”
