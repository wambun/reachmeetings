## Julie Barschow   Sales & Marketing Specialist

![Julie Barschow.png](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/28cd78_14fd7b40973340169dd05e3d036b0057~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_205,h_301,al_c,q_85,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/Julie%20Barschow.png)

Thank you for taking a few moments to read about me and the fabulous team at Reach! My love for uncovering magical moments through events began with planning special milestone occasions for loved ones and grew with my career in nonprofit development. I had the honor of helping raise millions of dollars through philanthropic events for the Boys & Girls Clubs of Austin and the YMCA of Austin. Taking the role as a servant leader and coming to know such inspiring stories along the way has been such a blessing.

I specialize in venue sourcing and hotel bookings for individuals and groups of any size. From weddings to vacations to corporate events, I would love to help you find the perfect location to meet your goals and make your memories! As a native of Austin and the hill country, I'm your go-to gal for the Hays County/hill country area, especially!

In my spare time, I enjoy exploring outdoors with my husband and our two kiddos, singing and playing guitar, and growing and harvesting food in my backyard garden in Buda.
